Second Source

Article-level analysis of how Florida politics gets covered.

Launching March 2, 2027, the opening of Florida’s regular session.

Method

Coverage is scored blind. Outlet name, byline, URL, and identifying boilerplate are stripped before any text reaches the model, so a score reflects the article rather than its masthead.

Published output is computed counts and verbatim quotes with links back to the original. Nothing is republished here, and no summary is written on a publication’s behalf.

Before launch

The engine is not considered working until it clears three tests:

  • Wire copy from the News Service of Florida scores near‑neutral.
  • Outlets separate in expected directions, with symmetric magnitude.
  • Swapping party names and actor identities reverses direction without changing magnitude.

Results of those tests will be published, including run‑to‑run variance.

Rubric and source code are public at github.com/nrhhds/SecondSource.
